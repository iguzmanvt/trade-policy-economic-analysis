# Evidence before sign-off in loss and damage finance

Isaac Guzmán Valdivia Tovar · Independent analytical work sample · 26 October 2026

## The operational question

How can an analyst help accelerate review while keeping the basis for a funding decision clear? My proposed approach is to organize the review around three questions: which policy applies, what evidence resolves the material risks, and what financial obligations the proposed design creates. A short, traceable record is useful when different specialists must reach a common recommendation under time pressure.

The Governing Instrument combines streamlined access with fiduciary safeguards and country ownership. It also places debt sustainability within the mandate for financial instruments. These principles provide the starting point; they do not determine the acceptability of an individual proposal. [GI, paragraphs 41, 43–50 and 57–59](https://unfccc.int/sites/default/files/resource/cp2023_11a01E.pdf).

## Distinguish authority from evidence

The policy register separates adopted frameworks, mandates, reported operating scope and unfinished policy development. The 2026 Board report confirms adoption of direct budget support review arrangements and the BIM results framework. It records that key funding-agreement elements were endorsed while finalization remained a later task. The report covers developments through July 2026, although UNFCCC published it in October. [2026 Board report, paragraphs 5 and 10(a), (d), (h)](https://unfccc.int/sites/default/files/resource/cp2026_07_cma2016_12_adv.pdf).

I would therefore attach a policy version and decision locator to each material review point. An attractive draft provision can inform design, but cannot establish an existing obligation. Likewise, an adopted framework does not demonstrate that a particular ministry has completed the required process. Before an operational review, subsequent decisions and the current request documents would need to be checked.

## Make the evidence request proportionate

Decision B.8/D.3 provides for project or programme risk review. Its annexes distinguish use of existing external assessments from proportionate ad hoc assessment, whose purpose includes identifying mitigation rather than imposing a simple pass or fail result. [B.8 decisions, page 7, Annex I page 14 and Annex III page 21](https://www.frld.org/sites/default/files/FRLD_B.8_19_Decisions%20of%20the%20Board_eighth%20meeting%20of%20the%20Board%2022-24%20April%202026.pdf).

The matrix translates this into an analyst's working method: record the assessed entity, evidence date, activity coverage, unresolved condition and responsible reviewer. For example, a prior assessment may be useful while leaving a new delivery channel unexamined. The next step would be a targeted evidence request tied to that gap. A missing document should remain a documented uncertainty; it should not become an unsupported accusation or disappear inside an average score.

## Keep financial design and results in the right decision

For an exploratory instrument comparison, I would show cash-flow timing, currency exposure, repayment and contingent liabilities separately. A high grant element does not establish sustainable debt, and a guarantee's face value does not equal a realized fiscal cost. The 2024 instruments paper supplies background options, not an adopted menu of products. [FLD/B.2/12, cover and pages 5–8](https://unfccc.int/sites/default/files/resource/LDF_Financial_Instruments.pdf).

The BIM is described as grant interventions in the 2026 report. Its results indicators are expressly separate from eligibility, selection and approval criteria. [2026 Board report, guidance table point 2 and paragraph 10(d)](https://unfccc.int/sites/default/files/resource/cp2026_07_cma2016_12_adv.pdf). These boundaries matter when presenting hypothetical loan scenarios or estimated benefits: neither should be mistaken for an available BIM product or a sufficient approval rationale.

The proposed handoff is a brief decision note stating the applicable authority, the economic case, evidence gaps, mitigation owners and outstanding decisions. Financial comparisons should also state which non-economic losses and distributional effects remain outside the calculation. The matrix supports that discussion; authorized institutions retain every funding and equivalency decision.
