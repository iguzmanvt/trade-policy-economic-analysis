# Reading FRLD resource and pipeline figures for a funding assessment

**Independent analytical note · 26 October 2026**

A funding assessment needs to identify which financial stage each number represents before comparing resources with requests. This reconciliation separates the stages and identifies the evidence still needed for a decision. The source is the FRLD Board's advance annual report dated 19 October 2026; its resource snapshot is **31 July 2026**, not the date of this analysis. [1]

## What the report establishes

| Measure | Reported value | Locator in report |
|---|---:|---|
| Pledges | USD 822.71 million | Printed p. 13, para. 17 |
| Received, body | USD 485.77 million | Printed p. 13, para. 17 |
| Received, summary | USD 485.97 million | Printed p. 3, para. 5 |
| Contributors with signed agreements | 25 contributors | Printed p. 13, para. 18 |
| Requests received / pipeline / non-compliant | 198 / 176 / 22 | Printed p. 13, para. 19 |
| Pipeline amount requested | USD 2.8 billion, rounded | Printed p. 13, para. 20 |
| Revised funding-request allocation | USD 342 million | Printed p. 10, para. 8 |
| Oversight provision | USD 28 million, separate | Printed p. 11, para. 10(c) |

The source reports two amounts received. Retaining both yields a **59.05%–59.07% received-to-pledges ratio** and an unresolved **USD 0.20 million difference**. Neither figure has been silently corrected or averaged. This small discrepancy changes little about the descriptive ratio, but it warrants a trustee reconciliation before the figure is used in an operational financial statement.

## Reconcile like with like

The intake cohort reconciles as **198 = 176 + 22**. Its pipeline-entry share is **88.89%**; this is not an approval rate. The original funding-request allocation of USD 250 million plus its USD 92 million increase reconciles to USD 342 million. Oversight is separate, and the supplemental USD 8 million oversight increase is already contained in the USD 28 million total. [1, paras. 8, 10(c), 10(k), 19]

The rounded requested amount is approximately **8.2 times** the funding-request allocation. That comparison indicates the scale of requests relative to this envelope; it does not measure approved financing needs, a legally committed shortfall, or country losses. The output table retains two decimal places for reproducibility, without implying additional precision in the USD 2.8 billion source value.

## Evidence needed for a financial recommendation

| Decision question | Additional evidence needed |
|---|---|
| How much is contractually due, and when? | Agreement amounts, conditions and dated payment schedules |
| What has been received as cash? | Reconciliation of cash and any promissory notes or other instruments |
| What can support new commitments? | Trustee commitment-authority statement under the applicable financial basis |
| What has already been committed or transferred? | Matched approval, commitment and cash-transfer registers |
| Which requests are ready to finance? | Project assessments, conditions and expected implementation/disbursement schedules |

The difference between pledges and either receipt figure is USD 336.74–336.94 million. Calling this difference arrears would require evidence of signed obligations and missed due dates. Similarly, a count of signed contributors does not establish the dollar value of their agreements.

The trustee glossary distinguishes a donor's pledge, an agreement, a paid contribution, an allocation, a commitment and a cash transfer. Paid contributions may include accepted non-cash instruments; unpaid amounts may be future-due or past-due. [2] These definitions guide the review, but they do not establish the actual composition of the FRLD figures. A missing value in this bounded extract is therefore left blank, not replaced with zero.
