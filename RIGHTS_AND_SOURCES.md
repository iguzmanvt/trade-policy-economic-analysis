# Rights and sources

Isaac Guzmán Valdivia Tovar prepared the new analytical text and portfolio code. This repository contains bounded public-statistics extracts, original summaries of public institutional reports, and an explicitly illustrative simulation. It excludes employer/client reports, correspondence, original client-project scripts, student records, application documents and personal contact details.

Public accessibility is not a blanket license. No repository-wide open-source license is assigned in this release. Respect the terms and attribution of each underlying source; inclusion does not transfer rights in third-party materials. The public input extracts remain attributable to their statistical or documentary publishers. See each project's source register for exact links, units, vintage, locators and limitations.

| Project | Source material and treatment |
|---|---|
| Observed duty intensity | Small numerical extract from archived U.S. Census Bureau statistics; new analysis code and prose |
| Trade concentration | Public U.S. Census partner-flow statistics; new all-goods extract and calculations, excluding the prior custom product selection |
| National Trade Estimate reports | Original factual coding and short paraphrases with official U.S. Trade Representative report/page links; no client corpus or full reports copied |
| Mexico input–output | Selected official National Institute of Statistics and Geography (INEGI) cells and attribution; independently written accounting analysis |
| Growth scenarios | New implementation of a standard Solow teaching exercise, with a pinned reference to the existing public course repository; no original teaching files copied or relicensed |

The portfolio is independent work. It implies no endorsement by the International Monetary Fund or any underlying source institution. Descriptive statistics, documentary coding and simulations are identified separately; none is presented as a causal trade-policy estimate.
